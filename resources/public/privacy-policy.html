Sibau Wars – Privacy

Sibau Wars is a free, non-commercial hobby game. It has no accounts, no ads, no payments and no analytics or tracking scripts.

What the game server handles

  • Your username and clan tag, which other players in your match can see.
  • Your IP address, which is used only to connect you to matches and to limit abuse (for example, too many connections from one address). It may show up in short-lived server logs.
  • A random player ID stored in your browser, so you can reconnect to a match after a refresh.

What your browser stores

Settings (volume, graphics, keybinds) and your last username are saved in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Hosting

The server is run by whoever hosts this copy of Sibau Wars, and their hosting provider may keep its own access logs.
